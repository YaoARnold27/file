# To Search Route Between Two Intersections:
    1. Define your mode of transportation - The walking + driving mode will save you travel time by allowing you to walk to an      intermediate     intersection for easy pickup
    2. Either click on the two intersections or search them up to input your start and end point
    3. The detailed instructions will then be displayed on the left panel. You can also see the optimal route highlighted on the mapQDQ
